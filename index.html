How totals work

Paid jobs automatically create or update an income entry in Money. Mark a job unpaid and its linked income is removed; deleting a job removes its linked income too. Do not manually add income for a job already marked paid, or it will be counted twice. When you give money to Mom, choose “For Mom / Savings”: it counts as an expense and appears in its own total.

Recurring reminders stay active until you pause or delete them. This standalone file shows reminders when opened; background notifications require a hosted installable version and notification setup.
